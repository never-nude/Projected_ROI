What it costs to launch ThreadKeep —
and what it could return.

Three funding scenarios over five years. The cost side is drawn directly from the conservative launch budget; the revenue side is a modeled range, with the reasoning laid out below.

All three funding tiers recover their investment during year two and finish year five well into profit. Modeled five-year ROI runs from ~467% (mid) to ~689% (high). The high tier returns the most both in dollars and as a percentage, because its added legal and security spend is what credibly opens the less price-sensitive professional market.

How the projection was built

Costs — grounded
  • Source. Every cost figure comes from the budget tab's "Conservative (NY) Revision," which already pads NY-metro rates and adds a 20% contingency. Low = $6,336 one-time; High = $26,640.
  • Build burn. AI subscriptions (Claude Max $200 + ChatGPT Pro $100 = $300/mo) over a 6-month build add $1,800. The sheet flags build duration as the dominant controllable cost — every extra month is +$300.
  • It doesn't end at ship. Annual recurring runs $99 (Apple renewal alone) to $4,599 (renewal + OS-compatibility passes + periodic security re-review for a tool holding full message histories).
Revenue — reasoned, not guessed blindly
  • Price. $18.99 list, netted to $16.50 after payment/merchant-of-record fees and a small refund allowance.
  • A paid market already exists. Competitor ChatKeepsake sells at ~$10 and does less — person-search only, no full-text search or JSON export — leaving clear room to price above it.
  • What each tier buys. Low = organic only: a founder-built landing page, word of mouth, light SEO. Mid = content/SEO plus a few Mac-press and review placements. High = the legal (privilege/HIPAA) and security reviews that make it credible to market to lawyers and doctors — a segment far less price-sensitive than consumers — plus PR. That professional reach is why the high tier's percentage return, not just its dollars, comes out ahead.

Key sensitivities & risks

  • Price is the biggest lever. The backlog also floats $8.99. At that price (net ~$7.80) revenue and profit roughly halve and break-even slips later into year 2 — still profitable, but thinner.
  • Demand is the real unknown. ThreadKeep is pre-launch; these are scenarios, not a forecast. A ±50% swing in sales moves the outcome proportionally. The growth curve — how fast monthly sales climb — is the single most influential input.
  • Execution capacity. The risk register's top item is the single-developer bottleneck across QA, security, legal, marketing, and support. The mid and high upside depends on sustaining a marketing cadence one founder can realistically run — which is precisely where an operations partner changes the math.
